Inputs and native settings

Scene/input changes reload the controlled configuration. A/B switches reset both histories. Split mode uses identical full-viewport UVs.

Half pipeline reduces geometry, denoising and history too, then upsamples the result. It can lose thin/contact detail; Full reconstruction preserves the existing mode.

These presets change only depth-sample count, not resolution, history length or reconstruction filtering. High/Ultra can look very similar after denoising; compare with Temporal disabled or during motion. Fewer samples do not imply a proportional reduction in total task time.

PR839 display intensity is scaled by 3. Native shaping is preserved: XeGTAO radius multiplier; PR839 bias 0.05 and power 1.5. Equal radius is not equal perceived strength. Temporal off still runs PR839's resolve/copy at weight 1.

Lit view applies the same whole-image multiply to both outputs. It does not compare the implementations' different native lighting integration.

Motion pruning skips unnecessary transport searches. Half pipeline reduces temporal work; Half with Full reconstruction only reduces estimation.
